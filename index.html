05 · Methodology & Data Notes
Administrative filtering: Enumeration-area boundaries are filtered using the available administrative attributes.

Area statistics: Area is calculated from the selected boundary geometries. Population and household totals are aggregated from the available EA attributes.

STAC discovery: Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 searches retrieve catalogue metadata and scene records. They do not automatically render satellite raster imagery.

Raster visualisation: NASA GIBS provides MODIS imagery tiles. These are global visualisation products and should not be interpreted as 10 m Sentinel-2 analysis products.

AOI restriction: The raster mask visually hides imagery outside the selected boundary. It does not perform pixel-level scientific clipping or alter the source raster.

Weather: Point weather data are requested from Open-Meteo.

Soil: Soil property information is requested from the ISRIC SoilGrids API where available.
